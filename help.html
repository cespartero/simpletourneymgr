How up and down works

Everyone plays doubles, and every round starts and ends at the same time. After each round:

  • Winners move one court toward court 1.
  • Losers move one court away from court 1.
  • Winners on court 1 stay on court 1. Losers on the last court stay there.
  • Partners change every round. Where a court gets a winner and a loser coming in, they're paired together. Otherwise last round's partners are split up.

Losers go to court 2

Over the night, the strongest players work their way to court 1. The board calls out anyone who has held court 1 for several rounds in a row.

For players

  1. Type your name in the Player List box and tap Join.
  2. Wait for the admin to start. You'll be placed on a court in list order, starting from court 1.
  3. Check the board each round to see your court and partner.

Entries close once the tournament starts. Make sure everyone has joined first.

Starting a tournament

  1. Get everyone onto the player list. You need at least 4 players.
  2. Tap Start session and enter the admin PIN. The first time, you'll choose a PIN.
  3. Choose the round timing:
    • Untimed: each round ends when every court has a result.
    • Timed: every round runs for a set time (minutes and seconds) after a 10 second setup.
  4. Choose the rounds to play. The suggested numbers, where everyone ends on the same number of games, are one tap away, but you can pick any number from 1 to 30. The round bar then shows, for example, Round 3 of 8.
  5. Check the summary. It tells you how many courts will be used and, if the numbers don't divide by 4, how many sit out each round.
  6. Tap Start up and down, then Start round 1 once everyone is on court.

Odd numbers and sit-outs

Each court takes 4 players. Anyone left over sits out that round, and sit-outs take turns. The app tells you how many rounds it takes for everyone to have played the same number of games, so you can plan a fair finish.

How long will it take?

Before you start, the Time estimator under the Round estimator in the player list panel works out roughly how long timed rounds will take. Pick the number of rounds (the suggested ones are one tap away) and the time per round. It shows the total, including the 10 second setup before each round, and about when you'd finish if you started now. Allow extra time between rounds for entering results and changing courts.

Running rounds

Start round
Begins the round. In timed mode this starts the 10 second setup, then the countdown, with beeps and a spoken countdown near the end.
Pause and Resume
Stops and restarts the clock in timed mode.
End round
Finishes the round early so you can enter results.
Round time
In timed mode, you can change the time for the next round before you start it.
Planned rounds
Once the planned number of rounds has been played, starting another round asks you first and offers to end the session instead.
Level rounds
When some players sit out each round, games only even out every few rounds. The round bar shows when everyone will next have played the same number of games, such as Games level after round 10. If everyone is level and you start another round, the app asks you to confirm first, tells you which round games will next be level, and offers to end the session instead.
Shuffle courts
Before a round starts, puts everyone due to play on a random court with random partners. Players due to sit out keep sitting out.
Page tabs
The tabs along the top switch between views: Board, and while a session is running Courts, Leaderboard and Timer, plus Help. Tap Full screen at the end of the tabs to fill the screen, handy for a TV.
Courts tab
Shows every court in the layout set with Arrange courts, with team names large enough to read from across the hall, the round clock, and who is sitting out. It updates live as results come in.
Leaderboard tab
Shows the tournament leaderboard so far, large: wins, losses, points difference, average points per game, win percentage and current streaks.
Timer tab
Shows a large timer on its own, handy for a screen beside the courts.

The page tab you're on is chosen per device, so a TV can show the Courts tab while your phone shows the Board. Esc takes you back to the Board. On phones and tablets, tap the screen once before the round starts so the browser allows the beeps and voice to play.

Entering scores

  1. When a round ends, enter the score for every court.
  2. The higher score wins and moves up.
  3. If a court finishes level, choose which team moves up. Both teams record a draw.
  4. Tap Save all results. The next round is set automatically.

Every court needs a result before the next round can begin. Made a mistake? Use Undo to step back the last action.

Standings and results

Allocations
Who is on which court this round, who is sitting out, and where everyone played last round.
Stats
Games played, won, drawn and lost, points won and lost, points difference, average points per game (Avg pts) and win percentage.
Streaks
Each player's current streak, best winning run and last 5 results.
History
Every match recorded this session, newest first: round, court, both teams and the score. After the session ends it's kept under the final leaderboard, in Match history.

When you end the session, the final results stay on the board. Use Copy results to paste them into a message or post, and Show last results to bring them back later.

Admin tools

Tap Admin and enter the PIN. Admin stays unlocked for 30 minutes, then locks again on its own.

Player List
Before the tournament, drag players to reorder the player list, use Sit out and Back in, or Remove someone.
Arrange courts
Set the number of columns and rows, then drag courts so the board matches the layout of your venue. Leave gaps for walkways.
Change PIN
Set a new admin PIN.
Re-add last session's players
Between sessions, puts everyone from the last session back on the player list in one tap. Anyone already on the list is skipped.
Add test players
Fill the player list with sample names to try the app out.
Download backup and Restore backup
Save the board to a file, or load one back in.
Start new session
Clear the last results and get ready for a new tournament.
Full reset
Wipes everything, including players and settings. Type RESET to confirm.
Lock admin
Locks admin straight away.

To finish for the night, tap End session. The final results stay on the board.

Where your data is kept

  • Everything is saved in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and no sign-in is needed.
  • Tabs open in the same browser stay in sync. Other devices each have their own separate board.
  • Clearing your browser's data clears the board. Use Download backup to keep a copy.
  • The app works offline. Download index.html (and this help page, if you want it) and open it in your browser.

Common questions

Can someone join once the tournament has started?
No. Entries close when it starts. End the session and start a new one to add people.
Why can't I start the next round?
Every court needs a result first. Check for a court with no score, or a draw where nobody has been chosen to move up.
I forgot the admin PIN.
Tap Admin, then Forgot the PIN? under the PIN box. The app owner can enter their override PIN there to reset the board, which also clears the admin PIN so a new one can be set. This wipes the session, player list and saved results too.
The beeps or voice don't play.
Turn the device volume up, tap the screen before starting the round, and keep the screen on. Some phones pause sound when the screen locks.
